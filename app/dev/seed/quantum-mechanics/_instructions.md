Write in plain English for a reader who knows high-school physics and algebra but no quantum mechanics.

- Introduce each idea in words before its formula, and say what the formula means.
- Write math as TeX with dollar signs: `$…$` inside a sentence, `$$…$$` on its own lines.
- Use SI units, and $\hbar$ rather than $h$ wherever either would do.
- Link to other pages by id, so the links survive a move.
