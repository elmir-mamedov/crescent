A measurement in quantum mechanics doesn't reveal a value the system already had. It gives one of the possible results at random, with odds set by the [[id:2m0eeje0u87ok|wavefunction]], and leaves the system in the state that matches the result. Measure the same quantity again straight away and you get the same answer.

This section covers two consequences:

- [[id:2yq61leljl3l6]]: some pairs of quantities can't both be sharp, however good the instrument
- [[id:1hltrthu6sbtk]]: a measurement with only two possible results, and what measuring in sequence shows
