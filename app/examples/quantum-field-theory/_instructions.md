Write for a reader who knows high-school physics and a little calculus, but no quantum field theory.

- Introduce each idea in words before its formula, and say what the formula means.
- Write math as TeX with dollar signs: `$…$` inside a sentence, `$$…$$` on its own lines.
- Keep $\hbar$ and $c$ in formulas until a page says it sets them to 1, and say so when it does.
- Give measured values with their source, and link the source under "Sources" at the end of the page.
- Link to other pages by id, so the links survive a move.
