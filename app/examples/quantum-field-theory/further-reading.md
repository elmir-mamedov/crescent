These pages give the ideas. To learn to calculate, these are the usual next steps, roughly from gentlest to hardest.

- **Richard Feynman, *QED: The Strange Theory of Light and Matter*** (1985). Four lectures for a general audience, with no equations, by the man who drew the diagrams.
- **Tom Lancaster and Stephen Blundell, *Quantum Field Theory for the Gifted Amateur*** (2014). Written for readers who have done undergraduate quantum mechanics, in short chapters.
- **David Tong, [Lectures on Quantum Field Theory](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qft.html)**. Free Cambridge lecture notes, clear and well loved.
- **Anthony Zee, *Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell*** (2nd edition, 2010). Intuition first, in a lively style.
- **Michael Peskin and Daniel Schroeder, *An Introduction to Quantum Field Theory*** (1995). The standard graduate course.
- **Matthew Schwartz, *Quantum Field Theory and the Standard Model*** (2014). A modern graduate course, through to the Higgs.
