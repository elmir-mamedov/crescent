The four pages here take one step each. Relativity and quantum mechanics together make the number of particles change, which a wavefunction of fixed particles can't follow, so the world must be built of fields. A field is a set of coupled oscillators. A quantum oscillator's energy comes in equal steps. And each step, in a field, is a particle.
