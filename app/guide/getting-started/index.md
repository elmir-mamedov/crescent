crescent is a knowledge base that lives on your computer. Your pages are Markdown files in a folder you own, and crescent shows them, lets you edit them, and remembers every version.

## The example space and the tour

A new knowledge base starts with an example space, **Quantum Field Theory**: real pages that use what this guide describes, from formulas to an interactive figure. It's yours like any other space, so change it or delete it when you no longer need it.

The first time crescent opens, a short tour walks you around that space, showing where everything is. **Take the tour**, here in Help, walks you around the page you're on again.

This guide lives in Help, where it can't be changed or deleted, and every update of crescent brings it up to date.
