## Notes

Select some text on a page, and crescent offers three kinds of note for it:

- **Highlight**: marks a phrase worth remembering.
- **Remark**: a comment about the passage.
- **Task**: something to change on the page later. The **Dashboards** button in the top-right corner collects the open tasks of a space.

An AI working on your pages can be asked to "address my notes": it makes the changes your tasks ask for and removes those tasks.

## Labels

Labels are coloured markers, such as "Draft" or "To revise", that show at the top of a page. Add one from the **Labels** item in a page's **⋯** menu. The **Dashboards** button lists the pages carrying a label, and the sidebar's search can be narrowed to pages with a label.
