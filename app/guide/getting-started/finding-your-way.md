## Search

The search box at the top of the sidebar (⌘/Ctrl + K) searches the space you are in, by title and by text. **Show all results** lists every match.

To find a word on the page you are reading, press ⌘/Ctrl + F.

## Tabs

⌘/Ctrl + click a page in the sidebar, or any link to one, to open it in a tab along the top. The arrows at the top left go back and forward between the pages you opened, as in a web browser.

## New pages

A glowing blue dot beside a page in the sidebar means it is new and you haven't opened it yet, whether you or an AI added it. Opening the page clears the dot; **Mark as unread** in the page's **⋯** menu puts it back.

## Long pages

A long page gets an **On this page** list beside it, which follows you as you scroll.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| ⌘/Ctrl + K | Search this space |
| ⌘/Ctrl + F | Find text on this page |
| ⌘/Ctrl + E | Edit this page |
| ⌘/Ctrl + S | Save the page you are editing |
| ⌘/Ctrl + click | Open a page in a new tab |
| ⌘/Ctrl + W | Close the tab you are on |
| Esc | Close search results, the find bar or a dialog |

**Help**, in the top-right corner, lists them all.
