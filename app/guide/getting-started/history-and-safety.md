## Every version is kept

Each time a page is saved, by you or by an AI, crescent records that version. **Diff**, at the top of a page, shows what changed between versions, so nothing you write is ever lost by an edit.

## Archive or delete

**Archive**, in a page's **⋯** menu, hides a page without deleting it, and it can be restored later. **Delete** removes the page from the space. A whole space can be archived or deleted in the same way from the spaces page.

## Downloads

**Download**, at the top of a page, saves it as a PDF or as a Markdown file. A page with images downloads as a zip of the Markdown and its images.

## Your pages, your folder

Your pages are plain files in a folder on your computer, `Crescent` in your home folder unless you chose another. You can back that folder up like any other.
