The **chat** button in the top-right corner opens a panel beside the page. It answers questions about your pages and, when you ask, writes or changes them.

The chat runs Claude Code or Codex, if one of them is installed on your computer and signed in, and its use counts against that account's plan.

## Having pages written

The chat answers in the panel unless you ask for a page: "write a page about…", "add this to the KB", or "write it down" after an answer. Switch on **Write to KB** under the message box to have every answer written as a page. New pages go into the space you are viewing, and when the chat finishes it lists every page it created or changed.

## Showing it an image

Attach an image with the paperclip under the message box, or drop it onto the chat. The chat can describe it, or put it on a page.

## Space instructions

The **Space instructions** button holds standing orders for that space, such as "write in Russian" or "keep pages short". Every AI that writes in the space follows them: the chat, and the Claude app and Claude Code too ([[id:3h7al7v5pv7rj]]).
